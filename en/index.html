What, exactly, is a "Jarvis"?

💬

More than a chatbot

A chatbot responds. A Jarvis acts: reads your calendar, sends messages, researches, organizes — and can do it on its own.

🧩

A system, not an app

Just as an operating system organizes programs and memory, a AI ONLY organizes the model, memory, tools, and channels.

🔓

Yours, for real

You can run it on your computer, with your data at home, without a monthly fee—you own the intelligence, you don't rent it.

No jargon. You don’t need to know how to code or anything about AI. Track 1 starts by explaining every word—"model," "agent," "tool"—with everyday examples. The rest of the course builds up one step at a time.
